How it works

Same public-overreaction idea as the NFL ATS model, adapted for a multi-game week:

  1. After each game, measure how far each team finished relative to the closing spread.
  2. A team’s score is its average ATS margin over the trailing 7 days.
  3. For today’s slate, rank matchups by the absolute gap between the two teams’ 7-day scores.
  4. Bet the underperformer (lower / more negative average). Publish the top 3 gaps.

Research tool only—not betting advice.
